Who BTF is

According to its official website, Build the Future Educational Consult is an educational consulting and EdTech organisation strengthening education in Ghana and beyond through consultancy, technology, teacher training and curriculum support, with study-abroad guidance as a complementary service.

BTF is a consultancy. It is not a school or university.
