Work & Visas

Six pathways, six different rule books

Visa eligibility depends on the destination, your circumstances and the current rules.

1. Studying in another country

Study permission is tied to an admission offer and the destination's student visa rules.

2. Seeking employment abroad

Finding legitimate employers and understanding the right to work in that country come before any travel.

3. Student work rights

Many countries allow limited work during study, with conditions that differ and change. Read the official page for your destination.

4. Employer-sponsored pathways

Some work visas require an employer to sponsor you. Genuine employers do not charge candidates for sponsorship.

5. Visitor and temporary visas

Visitor visas are generally for short stays and do not usually allow work or study. Always check the permitted activities.

6. Long-term residence pathways

Residence routes follow separate, detailed rules that depend on your circumstances. Use official government guidance.

Please note: BTF provides educational consultancy. Information here is general and rules change. Always confirm requirements with the relevant government authority or institution. BTF cannot guarantee admission, scholarships, visas, employment or residence.

Recognise and avoid scams

  • Guarantees of a visa, job or admission are a red flag.
  • Payments requested to personal accounts or by mobile money to individuals.
  • Pressure to decide today, or secrecy about the employer.
  • No verifiable company registration or official contact details.
  • Official visa fees are paid to government portals. Agent fees should be separate, written and itemised.
  • Verify the employer, read the whole contract and keep copies.
  • Use official immigration websites for your destination: see the Destinations page.
  • Keep your CV factual, with accurate dates and references.
