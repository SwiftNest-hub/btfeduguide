Documents Academy

The documents behind an application

General guidance only. Institutions and countries set their own lists.

Preparation checklist

Progress is saved only in this browser on this device. Clearing browsing data removes it.

Academic transcripts

An official record of courses and grades, issued by your school or university. Institutions use it to judge academic readiness. Request it early and check if it must be sent directly. Common mistake: submitting an unofficial copy. Requirements vary.

Certificates

Evidence of qualifications you completed, issued by the awarding body or institution. Check whether copies must be certified.

Passport

Identity and travel document issued by your government. Check expiry dates against your planned stay. Renewals can take time.

CV or résumé

A concise summary of education, experience and skills that you write. Keep it accurate and tailored to the programme or role.

Statement of purpose

Your own essay on why this programme and what you will do with it. Avoid generic text. Be specific and honest.

Motivation letter

Similar to a statement of purpose, often shorter and more direct. Read the programme's instructions on format.

Recommendation letters

Written by teachers or supervisors who know your work. Ask early, share your goals, and follow how the institution wants them submitted.

Proof of funds

Evidence you can cover tuition and living costs, in the form the authority specifies. Never invent or borrow funds for show. Authorities check.

English-language test results

Required by some institutions, issued by the test provider. Check accepted tests, minimum scores and validity period.

Admission letters

Issued by the institution when you are offered a place. Read conditions carefully.

Supporting documents for visa applications

Lists are set by the immigration authority and change. Use the official checklist for your case only.

Privacy: do not upload passports, bank statements or other sensitive documents to this website. It has no secure upload system. Share them only through official portals you have verified.
