Frequently asked questions

Can BTF guarantee admission or a visa?

No. Admission and visa decisions are made by institutions and governments. BTF provides educational guidance only.

Is the scholarship directory complete?

No. It lists selected official providers and is updated by BTF. Always check the provider's website for current details.

Do requirements change?

Yes, often. Use official sources before making any decision.
